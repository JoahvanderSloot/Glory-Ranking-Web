Welcome to my GLORY ELO Ranking System.

This platform is an independent project designed to provide a clear, data-driven ranking of GLORY kickboxers. Using an ELO-based system, fighters gain and lose points based on their performances, with factors such as opponent strength influencing the outcome. This creates a dynamic and continuously updating leaderboard across all active weight divisions.

The goal of this system is to offer a more objective and transparent way of tracking fighter performance over time, similar to ranking systems used in competitive sports and gaming.

This project was created and developed by Joah van der Sloot, a software developer from the Netherlands and a long-time GLORY fan. The idea originated from a desire to better understand and predict fight outcomes, starting during the Heavyweight Grand Prix in 2024. What began as a personal project has grown into a full ranking system covering the GLORY roster.

How the prediction engine works

This prediction page estimates fight outcomes by combining fighter Elo, win/loss history, KO rates, physical attributes, and style matchups. Together with calculated momentum and previous fight data. It is meant as a fun and data-informed guide rather than a guaranteed result.

The more accurate information you add for each fighter, the more reliable the simulation becomes. Unknown values are treated as neutral baseline inputs so the system can still produce an estimate.

These predictions are not meant for betting, gambling, or any real-world financial decision. Treat them as a reference tool for discussion and analysis.
